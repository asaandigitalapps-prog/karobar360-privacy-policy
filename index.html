1. Introduction

Karobar360 ("we," "our," or "us") is a business accounting and bookkeeping application developed by AsaanDigital. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application.

By using Karobar360, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. We will not use or share your information with anyone except as described herein.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of data to provide and improve our services:

Precise Location GPS-based precise location App functionality — branch auto-fill and geolocation features Optional

3. How We Use Your Information

All data collected is used solely for app functionality:

  • Contacts and Personal Info: Used only when you explicitly select a contact via the contact picker to create customer or vendor records within the app.
  • Location Data (Precise and Approximate): Used for branch auto-fill and geolocation features. We collect both precise (GPS) and approximate (network-based) location. Location access is requested at runtime with an in-app disclosure before first use.
  • Photos: Used for attaching invoice photos, receipt images, and business documents within the app.
  • Audio/Voice: Used for speech recognition to enable voice-based data entry. Audio is processed ephemerally — it is converted to text in real time and the raw audio recording is not stored.
  • Device Identifiers: Used to deliver push notifications, including payment reminders and app updates, via Firebase Cloud Messaging.

4. Biometric Authentication

Karobar360 supports fingerprint and biometric login for secure access. Biometric data is processed entirely on your device using Android's BiometricPrompt API. We do not collect, store, or transmit any biometric data to our servers.

5. SMS Share Feature

Karobar360 appears in Android's Share sheet for text content. This allows you to share bank SMS messages to the app for analysis. The app only receives messages you explicitly choose to share — we do not access, read, or request permission to your SMS inbox.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal data with any third parties.

Your data is not sold, rented, or transferred to third-party companies or organizations. Data is transmitted only between your device and our secure servers for the purpose of providing app functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect data as described in their respective privacy policies:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): Used for push notifications. Collects device identifiers. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services: Required for app distribution and updates. Google Privacy Policy

8. Data Security

We take the security of your data seriously:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. The app does not allow cleartext (unencrypted) network traffic.
  • Biometric Lock: App access can be secured with fingerprint or biometric authentication.
  • Code Protection: The app uses ProGuard/R8 for code obfuscation and shrinking to protect against reverse engineering.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide our services.

Account Deletion

You can request complete deletion of your account and all associated data. Upon deletion, we will remove all your personal information, business records, and any data stored on our servers.

Data Deletion Without Account Removal

You can also request deletion of specific data (such as activity history, transaction history, and images) without deleting your entire account.

To request deletion, visit: Account & Data Deletion

10. Children's Privacy

Karobar360 is designed for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 16. If we discover that a child under 16 has provided us with personal information, we will immediately delete it from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.
